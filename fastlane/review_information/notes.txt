BrewDesk is a free, accountless guide to working from cafés in New York City. No purchases, subscriptions, advertising, analytics, accounts, or user-generated content. No review credentials are required.

REVIEWING FROM OUTSIDE NYC: the dataset is NYC-focused by design. Wherever you are, the app shows the full NYC dataset — with or without location access. If you grant location outside New York, a banner notes you are outside coverage and the map stays on NYC. Nothing requires being in New York to review.

Location is optional. On the location-introduction screen, choose "Use Union Square instead" to review the full app without granting permission.

WHAT DISTINGUISHES THIS APP — claim-level evidence, not crowd ratings:
1. Open Explore and search for "Housing Works", then open Housing Works Bookstore Cafe.
2. The Workability section shows each Wi-Fi, outlet, laptop-policy, and noise claim with its source, confidence, and observation date. Estimates are labeled as estimates.
3. Every café card carries an "Updated <date> · <source>" stamp. A seal appears only where a human verified the claim.
4. Tap the info button (top-left of Nearby) for "How Work Fit is scored" — the complete scoring methodology is published inside the app: weights (laptop policy > seating > Wi-Fi = outlets > noise), confidence blending, and a 90-day freshness decay.

Work-specific data no general-purpose map shows: explicit laptop policy including time-limited/weekend/discouraged venues shown openly, seating availability, and honest "unknown" states — we never guess. Users can import their Google Maps saved places; the file is parsed on-device and never uploaded.

The production service is https://venuekit-ashen.vercel.app. Privacy, terms, support, and OpenStreetMap attribution are linked from the About tab.
