## Blocking questions

### What does BrewDesk do that all three do not?

BrewDesk exposes the source, confidence, and observation date behind each
Wi-Fi, outlet, laptop-policy, and noise claim. The composite Work Fit score is
evidence the user can inspect rather than an unexplained rating. BrewDesk also
models explicit laptop policy and focuses its editorial coverage on NYC.

### Is the differentiator in this build?

Yes. Venue details display claim-level source, confidence, and date. Estimates
are visibly labeled. The exact archived/TestFlight build must be checked again
before submission.

### Do the listing and screenshots lead with the differentiator?

Yes. Screenshot 1 shows venue-detail evidence, followed by work filters and the
Work Fit map. A generic map does not lead the set.

### Is this a near-duplicate of another Bamware app?

No. BrewDesk’s concept, data, interface, and workflow are unrelated to Baat.

### Does the build render on Apple’s likely review hardware?

Yes. The Release UI smoke test passed on an iPad Air 11-inch simulator in
iPhone compatibility mode on 2026-08-19.

## Decision

The evidence-first screenshot set and iPad compatibility check are complete.
Guideline 4.3 risk remains high because the map-plus-work-score proposition
already exists; provenance must remain visible and central in the submitted
screenshots and exact TestFlight build.
